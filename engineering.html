03

HVAC Chiller Testing Lab

Modbus RTU acquisition and certification reporting

Automates Sabro's chiller test bench. Polls six Modbus RTU slave devices on a one-second cycle for temperature, pressure, humidity and electrical load, then computes the numbers the lab actually certifies on — total and sensible BTU, latent capacity, EER, and superheat/subcool against the R410A pressure-temperature chart. Results export as labelled PDF reports.

Bus
Modbus RTU over serial, slave IDs 1–6, 1 s auto-poll
Sensors
S1–S4, A1–A8, superheat, subcool, wet bulb, enthalpy
Compute

Reporting
Landscape PDF via iTextSharp, grouped by circuit
