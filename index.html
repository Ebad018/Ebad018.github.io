Cultivation Fortress Sim

A Dwarf Fortress-inspired cultivation colony sim in modern C++20 with no game engine. SFML draws the pixels; every simulation, generation and UI system is hand-built under SOLID discipline.

Engine ArchitectureKept a 30k-line simulation maintainable and testable by decomposing it into a single authoritative World state driven by ~40 single-responsibility systems (CultivationSystem, PawnSystem, A* PathSystem, SiegeSystem) behind one SimEngine facade, keeping the SFML/ImGui front-end fully decoupled from a header-only, engine-agnostic core.
Procedural GenerationGenerated an endlessly varied, believable world by chaining a multi-pass pipeline (Perlin/FBM elevation → river carving → spirit-vein seeding → adjacency-ruled biomes → irregular corruption fronts), then folding it into a two-layer surface + underground tile grid threaded with minable qi veins.
Emergent History SimProduced Dwarf-Fortress-style living lore by simulating a century of pre-history before play begins: 75+ historical figures who forge grudges, break through cultivation realms, and forge named artifacts across ~500 recorded events the player later inhabits.
Data-Driven ContentTurned design documents into the live game database by authoring all content (beasts, weapons, arts, resources, events) as human-readable data tables and writing a custom parser that ingests those tables directly into typed C++ objects at startup: one source of truth shared by writer and engine.
Tooling & WorkflowEnabled fast, verifiable iteration on a headless-friendly build by engineering a CMake/VS2022 toolchain and a screenshot-based self-test harness that boots the game, drives scripted scenarios, and dumps annotated PNGs for automated visual regression checks.
C++20 · SFML / ImGui · A* Pathfinding · CMake
